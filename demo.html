What it shows

  • QR onboarding — link a WhatsApp Business number in about five minutes
  • Keyword-triggered auto-replies with reply-count tracking
  • Visual multi-step flow builder — no code required
  • Bulk broadcasts with recipient, sent, and delivery counts
  • AI replies that hand off to a human when a query needs a person
  • Shared team inbox with role-based access
How this was made. Every frame is my own build — the product interface, the demo data, and the video pipeline (rendering, captions, audio mix) are all things I built rather than stock footage or a third-party template. The voiceover is synthetic; I generated it locally per caption chunk so the burned captions line up with the spoken words exactly.
