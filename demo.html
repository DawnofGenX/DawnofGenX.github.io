What it shows

Six capabilities, each demonstrated on the product itself.

01

QR onboarding

Link a WhatsApp Business number by scanning a code — about five minutes, no developer needed.

02

Keyword auto-replies

Rules that fire on customer keywords, with reply counts so you can see what actually gets used.

03

Visual flow builder

Multi-step conversations assembled by dragging nodes onto a canvas. No code, no deploy pipeline.

04

Bulk broadcasts

Segmented campaigns with recipient, sent and delivery counts reported honestly.

05

AI replies with handoff

Answers routine questions day or night, then hands anything unusual to a person.

06

Shared team inbox

Role-based access, assignment and presence, so nothing falls between shifts.
